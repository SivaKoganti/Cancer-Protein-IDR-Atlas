INSTRUMENTATION & METHODS
================================================================================

Instrument Details:
  - Manufacturer: Perkin Elmer Corporation
  - Model: Spectrum 100 FT-IR Spectrometer with DTGS Detector
  - Light Source: Globar (ceramic) at 1200 K
  - Wavenumber Range: 400–4000 cm⁻¹
  - Resolution: 4 cm⁻¹
  - Beam Splitter: KBr (4000–650 cm⁻¹) / Mylar (600–400 cm⁻¹)
  - Detector: DTGS (Deuterated Triglycine Sulfate) with KBr window

Sample Preparation:
  - Method: Diffuse Reflectance (DR)
  - Sample Matrix: Spectroscopic grade KBr powder
  - Protein/KBr Ratio: 10 mg/ml protein in powdered KBr (5 mg sample per measurement)
  - Dilution: Protein solutions lyophilized then mixed with KBr
  - Cup: Diffuse Reflectance Accessory with rotating sample holder
  - Purging: Dry nitrogen gas (15 L/min)

Measurement Parameters:
  - Wavenumber Calibration: CO₂ reference line at 2345 cm⁻¹
  - Scan Speed: 2 cm/s (0.5 s per scan)
  - Number of Accumulations: 64 scans (averaging for S/N enhancement)
  - Apodization: Happ-Genzel function
  - Baseline: KBr powder (0.1 M PBS, lyophilized)
  - Atmospheric Compensation: Yes (H₂O and CO₂ subtraction)

Software Analysis:
  - Data Processing: Perkin Elmer Spectrum 10.3.08
  - Smoothing: Savitzky-Golay (window 9 points, polynomial 2)
  - Baseline Correction: Linear baseline between 3600–600 cm⁻¹
  - Deconvolution: Lorentzian curve-fitting using GRAMS/AI software
  - Peak Finding: Second derivative method, derivative threshold 0.5

Quality Control:
  - Blank (KBr): %R = ±0.5% (acceptable < ±1%)
  - Positive Control (α-helix protein): β-Lactoglobulin verified
  - Negative Control (random coil): Collagen (atypical, reference only)
  - Daily Wavenumber Calibration: ±0.1 cm⁻¹ using polystyrene standard
  - Spectral Quality Check: S/N ratio > 500:1 in amide I region
