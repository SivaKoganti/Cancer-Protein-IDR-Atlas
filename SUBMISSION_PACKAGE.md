## Submission-ready cover letter

Dear Editors,

We are pleased to submit our manuscript, "A residue-resolved atlas of disorder, phase separation, and virus-interface signals in cancer proteins," for consideration as a research article in PLOS Computational Biology.

This work presents a reproducible computational atlas that integrates intrinsic disorder, sequence-based phase-separation propensity, conservation, structural-confidence information, ClinVar-related context, and curated oncoviral interaction evidence across 50 cancer genes. By unifying these heterogeneous signals at single-residue resolution, we provide a practical framework for comparing regulatory flexibility, condensate-relevant sequence features, and virus-contact burden in a common analytical setting. We also introduce a transparent VIPP-based prioritization scheme and a compact structural library that make the resulting annotations interpretable and suitable for downstream experimental follow-up.

The study is motivated by the need for integrative, residue-level resources that connect cancer biology, disordered regulatory regions, and host-virus interface signals in a way that is directly interrogable and extensible. Our atlas is intended primarily as a resource for hypothesis generation and systematic prioritization rather than as a definitive mechanistic model, and we have framed the manuscript accordingly.

The analysis is supported by reproducible workflows, curated outputs, and figures that illustrate the atlas landscape, representative gene profiles, benchmarking behavior, PTM-stratified pathway differentials, and case-study residues. We believe the manuscript will be of interest to researchers working on protein disorder, phase separation, cancer biology, host-virus interactions, and computational approaches to biological prioritization.

During confidential review, data and code are available to editors and authorized reviewers upon request to the corresponding author, with full public release planned following publication and final licensing declaration.

We appreciate your consideration and look forward to the opportunity to contribute this work to the journal.

Sincerely,

Siva Koganti
